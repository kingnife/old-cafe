OUR STORY & HERITAGE

It all began with an old, decommissioned 1954 Probat cast-iron drum roaster and a simple conviction: coffee should be roasted with unhurried reverence, deep human touch, and uncompromised integrity.

Over sixteen years ago, our founders reclaimed an abandoned brick warehouse by the old rail yard. Today, that very drum roaster still hums every morning, filling the street with the unmistakable aroma of caramelized sugar, roasted nuts, and warm berries.

We reject the industrial homogenization of coffee. Every crop season has a unique character dictated by rainfall, soil composition, and altitude. Our responsibility as roasters is not to overpower these traits with scorch, but to unlock their purest natural sweetness.
