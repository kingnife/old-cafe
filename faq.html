Common Questions & Care Guides

Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?

All online orders are fulfilled using beans roasted within 24 to 48 hours of dispatch. Coffee beans benefit from a 3 to 5 day resting window after leaving the drum roaster to allow carbon dioxide to degas naturally, ensuring ideal extraction and sweetness in your cup.

What is the best way to store my roasted coffee beans?

Keep your coffee in its original foil-lined pouch with the one-way valve sealed tight, or in an opaque airtight ceramic canister stored at room temperature away from direct sunlight, heat, and moisture. Never store coffee in the refrigerator or freezer, as moisture condensation quickly degrades delicate aromatics.

Can I order pre-ground coffee for my brew method?

Yes! In our online shop, you can choose from Whole Bean, Coarse (French Press / Cold Brew), Medium (Drip / Chemex), and Fine (Espresso / Moka Pot). We grind using industrial precision flat burrs right before packaging.

How does the Roaster’s Circle subscription work?

Subscribers save 15% on every bag, receive free shipping, and get first access to rare micro-lot releases. You can easily adjust delivery frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly), skip an order, or cancel anytime through your order link.

Do offer decaffeinated options?

Yes! We offer a specialty Colombia Mountain Water Process decaf that preserves full body, cocoa notes, and honeyed sweetness without the use of chemical solvents.
